## Install or update

With **Node.js 24** installed, run these in the repository folder:

```sh
npm ci
npx playwright install --no-shell chromium
npm run verify
```

Open **Extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked** and select **dist**. In Codex, find extensions under **Settings → Browser → Extensions**. After an update, reload the extension and refresh your 1688 tabs.

## Import and prepare

1. Open your [1688 orders](https://work.1688.com/home/buyer.htm), tick the orders you want, and choose **Import selected**. Confirm the count and keep the list open while the import runs. The reader tries a background tab, then an unfocused window, then a visible tab automatically, according to what the browser allows. If 1688 asks for a sign-in or verification, the import pauses and the workspace shows one notice: choose **Open the 1688 tab**, finish the step, then **Resume import**. Older paused queues need **Resume** or a new **Refresh**; reopening the workspace does not restart saved bulk jobs.
2. Click **Open Workspace**. If a new tab is blocked, use **Open Workspace here** after the import finishes.
3. In **Orders**, filter purchase months with the month chips, choose orders, then select the product rows to pack. **Ctrl+A** in the product table selects the full filtered product list across orders.
4. In the action bar at the bottom, set **Packing month**, check the product/order count shown just above it, and choose **Prepare packing list**. Every newly assigned product—even a single product—first shows a review of its photos, packing month, proposed codes and naming/size/material helpers. Choose **Assign only** to save these values and stay in Orders with your selection intact, or **Assign codes and prepare** to continue to packing. Assign only is available when the selected sellers are identified and existing products are not moving months. Cancel leaves packing and code reservations unchanged. Reopening already assigned products is direct; moving existing codes also requires review.
5. Use **Organize** to arrange groups and products, review names and labels, then copy from the selection toolbar docked at the bottom of the packing list. **Changes save automatically**. Use **Copy… → Back up packing** when finished.

The **Orders** and **Packing list** tabs at the top switch views. **Packing list** reopens the products already assigned to the packing month without changing your Orders selection; **Orders** saves packing edits first, then returns. Purchase-month filters start with the current month and remember your choice across reloads. **Packing month** starts at the current month whenever the workspace opens; it is independent of purchase dates. October purchases can therefore receive December codes. Only the selected products move; a partly selected group splits so its unselected products keep their codes. With no product rows selected, **Prepare packing list** reopens products already assigned to that packing month, across saved orders. It does not select purchases by date.

The main Orders list and Products table show purchases newest first, including their purchase time within a day. Products retain their source order inside each order; missing purchase dates appear last. Each successful import refreshes the open workspace immediately. A new order matching the current filters returns Products and the Orders sidebar to the top, while filters and selected product rows remain intact. Your packing arrangement continues to control the preparation form and its exports independently.

Refresh checks that its reader tab actually exists before using it. If the browser returns a lost tab ID, the importer tries its next supported reader mode or reopens the missing reader; sign-in and genuine browser failures still pause with an actionable message. Unrelated tabs and the last good saved order remain intact.

Orders and Products scroll continuously through their complete filtered lists. Around 100 order cards and 100 product rows stay loaded in their respective lists; an active product editor remains available until you finish editing. Scrolling and resizing keep your place, selection and pending values. Month headings still expand or collapse their orders. Copying, photo navigation, printing and totals use the complete filtered or selected scope. Prepared product rows show and copy their full assigned code, including variant and size endings, in the standard rounded black badge with bold white text and the product-number underline.

For fees, price adjustments or other rows that should not enter packing, use **Skip packing** beside the product's preparation action. The compact icon appears on row hover or keyboard focus and stays visible on touch screens. Enter **Pcs/box** in the small field beside **Quantity** in either Orders → Products or Packing. It stays editable before and after assigning codes, including products with recorded cartons. It means the number of units intended for one full carton. Changes save automatically; blank means unknown, and only positive whole numbers are accepted. A known value has the suffix **/box** in English, **/ctn** in Spanish or **/箱** in Chinese; unknown values have no suffix. The adjacent calculated summary updates immediately: for product 1001 with **150 pieces**, enter **10** to see **15 calculated cartons**. | Ordered units / Pcs/box                | Result and review                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 150 / 10                               | 15 calculated cartons, immediately after entering 10.                                                         |
| 100 / 10                               | 10 full cartons; initialize 10 carton marks.                                                                  |

Record store label printing/application services and shipping-refund promises, order instructions and production delays, warehouse receipt and missing-label issues. A known feature name still needs its current cost. **Source payable** and **Source freight** can fill actual-amount fields after you check they match; missing source amounts offer no shortcut, and zero remains zero. Yuan amounts use readable grouping and two decimal places. All changes remain drafts until **Save workflow**.

**Product features → Add feature** accepts any named feature, such as lighting, rotation, engraving or waterproofing, with a cost in yuan per piece. **Additional cost** adds that amount to this product’s effective unit price and quantity-based packing value. **Already recorded** keeps the feature cost as information when the source price, linked payment or additional order charges already include it. ### Store spreadsheet orders

**Import spreadsheet** adds a new store or factory order from a marked `.xlsx` or `.xls` file. A numeric filename identifies a market store number; a company-name filename identifies a factory. Windows copy suffixes retain that store identity, and importing the same file again creates a separate order. Confirm the purchase date and store name, review pictures and cell-level issues, then import. New orders appear immediately in purchase-date order and use the same Prepare workflow as 1688 orders. Store names from spreadsheets can be edited in order details; **View all orders from this store** works for both sources. Imported orders have no fabricated 1688 links and are excluded from 1688 refresh.

Order details keeps store-name and **WeChat ID** fields beside compact Save buttons, preserving other unsaved fields when saving one value. WeChat is optional for every source, shared across orders with the same known store identity in the account, and cleared by saving an empty field. IDs are stored locally and in backups; the extension only copies them when requested and never contacts WeChat or sends them to a service. The generated **Imported** badge and these controls follow the selected interface language.

Import review uses a compact card grid with affected photos, group/row references and relevant values. Review acknowledgments, checked items and blocking errors have distinct colors. **Check all** acknowledges the review items that have checkboxes; arithmetic errors, missing pictures and variant quantities still require correction or manual allocation before import.

Add one row of **import markers** above the seller’s headers in a copy of the workbook. Only marked columns are read. The compact **Download template** icon beside Purchase date provides a ready workbook. The import popup keeps all instructions in a compact right sidebar with **Setup**, **Markers** and **Checks** tabs; marker cards copy their exact spelling. The guide and order review scroll independently, and the guide stacks below the form on narrow screens. The same guide is available in **Settings → Data → Store spreadsheet guide**.

The template has **Orders** and **Guide** sheets, with all English markers in place, 20 blank photo-ready product rows, frozen headings, formatted numeric inputs and quantity/amount formulas. Enter the supplier’s invoice total separately so import can compare it with the calculated line amounts. ## Keep your data

An order referenced by active cartons cannot be deleted from the library until those carton records are explicitly removed. This also protects a carton-mark owner whose products are not inside the carton. Removed carton records retain their deletion history.

Use **Settings → Data → Back up** for the complete library. Full-library backups now use version 4 with physical carton batches; versions 1–3 and existing compressed image archives still restore. **Copy… → Back up packing** exports the whole open preparation independently of selection, retaining source dependencies, current assignments, scoped checkpoints and label/photo data. It includes a batch only when all contents, its month and its actual carton-mark group are covered; `omittedCartonBatchIds` reports intersecting omissions. Full backups preserve mark owners that contribute no pieces. Newer revisions, explicit clears and carton deletion tombstones survive restore; an older backup cannot recreate a removed batch. Original supplier evidence stays intact during legacy conversion. Removed/unrelated products, unnecessary history, global vocabularies and unrelated assets remain outside scoped packing backups.

Both backup actions download **`.json.gz`** files: lossless compressed archives that store each identical picture once, retaining all original pixels and metadata. Restore accepts these files directly, as well as older `.json` backups; you do not need to unzip them. Keep the original file until your restored library is verified. Unique original photos still take space; this improves backup size without deleting source history or reducing picture quality. Database storage remains local and is unchanged by compression. Import reports and packing-list/print metadata continue to use their existing JSON formats.

Choose **Restore packing** (the upload icon after **Prepare packing list** in the Orders action bar) to review a packing file and open its saved scope without assigning new codes. This action is unavailable inside an active preparation. Newer local edits, unrelated packing products and existing global settings are kept; label settings can initialize an empty library. Saved preparation names and materials survive scoped restore, workspace reloads and reopening. Automatic names change when you explicitly edit their naming rules; unrelated settings changes keep the saved text. Full-library backups, including global naming/size/color rules and appearance, still belong in Settings. Restore rejects conflicting current ownership without partially importing the file; older backups cannot revive inactive assignments. Refreshes preserve edits. Use one buyer account per library.

Data and naming rules stay local; there is no online translation service. Beside the sun/moon appearance toggle, **A / 文 / Ñ** switches the interface between English, Chinese and Spanish and remembers your choice. Workspace display helpers follow this choice. Popup toggles independently control their dialog and exports without changing the workspace language or other popups. Original seller descriptions and saved label names remain unchanged. Switching language keeps open edits. The active light/dark mode, language, filters, copy choices and Activity read state are browser preferences, separate from library backups; a floating panel's minimized state lasts for its browser-tab session. Press **Escape** in Organize to return to normal packing; in normal packing it returns to Orders after autosave; unresolved errors keep your edits open. The detail-page **Download CSV** remains available.
