**Detailed Description** [REQUIRED — draft]

```text

Collect selected orders in the 1688 Packing workspace. In Orders, filter purchase months, dates, sellers, order numbers and product text. Purchase-month filters start with the current month and remember your choice. Shift-click filters a month range, while month headings collapse navigation. The summary above the products table counts complete matching orders once, including shipping and order discounts. A separate live selection summary shows selected rows, item quantities, price-times-quantity sums and amounts after saved row discounts; displayed and whole-order quantities provide context without prorating shipping.

Double-click a product or press Enter for original details, individual source-value copying, full-description copying, photos and order details. Unknown seller wording and source data remain unchanged.

Select products, using Ctrl+A for every filtered product across orders and pages, then choose Packing month. This month sets CE codes independently of purchase dates and starts at the current month whenever the workspace opens. Prepare packing list shows its scope before switching to the full packing view. Existing-code changes show affected photos and old/new codes for review. Only selected products move; partly selected groups split so other products keep their codes. With no products selected, preparation reopens products already assigned to the packing month.

Known products receive uppercase Spanish names and Chinese categories from local rules; unknown products stay unnamed. Suggestions are saved automatically and your manual names remain unchanged. Each group's product code, names, original title, code options and grouping tools stay visible. Named sizes and comparable measurements can receive size endings automatically; unclear combinations stay for review. Edit barcode and carton-mark text from their previews, zoom and pan photos, browse the current product photos with Previous/Next, and apply shared names to an order. Changes save automatically; Undo/Redo cover pending edits. Hover or focus a field for a short hint.

Each packing variant displays its source unit price and quantity without a discount field. Shared barcodes show the distinct unit prices and combined quantity of the included source rows. Material suggestions use clear seller wording; unknown materials stay blank and your manual edits take priority. Apply a material to a product, group or order. Preparation shows its current stage and product counts as names, sizes, colors and materials are processed. Double-click a packing product for copyable original data. Larger photos show a small copy icon on hover or keyboard focus. Selected groups use a restrained tint and a check mark. Hints disappear when the pointer leaves their field.

Click a row to select it and click again to deselect; Shift-click a range or Ctrl-click individual rows. Packing group clicks add or remove that group without clearing other groups. Double-click an order for details. Buttons and photos work independently. Drag product groups within their order and products within their group without changing codes. Reset order and codes restores the order's default arrangement and becomes unavailable when already at defaults; Restore previous packing restores its setup before the last month move. Remove group and Remove product affect packing only. These changes show affected products before applying. Source orders and issued codes stay safe. Conflicts appear while typing; inspect the original owner and photos, use the next available code or keep your saved version. Open selected order requires exactly one chosen order and reuses one tab. Click a seller name or order number to copy its full text.

Copy source values, separate pictures, chosen packing columns or printable labels into Excel. The packing view puts copy controls at the top and disables them when no group or product is selected. Ctrl+A selects all displayed packing products. Product codes appear once per group in preparation columns. Headers start off; both Headers and column choices are remembered. Copy Excel image uses the workspace language for headings and recognized attributes, categories and materials. Choose image columns and drag them or use arrows to reorder; the saved layout works in both views. Optional Supplier details columns leave carton dimensions, weight and carton numbers blank for suppliers. Category & material shows material in bold below the category. Consecutive variants sharing a carton mark use one merged cell with its actual image, without a repeated code caption. Included marks need a Chinese category for prepared codes; missing required data or failed images preserve the clipboard. Manually reordered barcode base codes appear blue. Large copies offer numbered batches. Copying has no confirmation warning; barcode and carton-mark pictures copy separately.

Refresh selected or matching orders. Multi-order import and refresh confirm their scope; Activity holds progress, pause/retry and Save import report. Successful saves survive retries, and refreshes preserve notes and packing edits. Settings contains Labels, Product names, Size codes and Data. HOW TO USE

Orders without seller information retain their products and totals, with a clear notice. Identify the seller before assigning label codes. Save an import report directly beside the order-page Pause/Resume controls or from Activity.
Select order checkboxes on your 1688 order list and choose Import selected. Confirm the scope and keep the list open while the reader tab works. Click Open Workspace, filter purchase months, choose orders and select products. Check Packing month, click Prepare packing list, arrange products, copy into Excel and save a packing backup. Open Workspace here is available if a new tab is blocked. The single-order CSV button remains available.

PRIVACY
Orders, versions, notes, preparation edits, issued codes, label settings, naming and size rules, selected logos and cached pictures stay in your browser. Back up and restore using a local file. Labels and suggestions are generated locally; no remote AI, online translation or developer service receives your orders, and no analytics are used. The extension does not read passwords/authentication cookies or submit, pay for, or alter orders. Importing opens 1688 pages using your existing session; product pictures may be requested from Alibaba CDN.

SCOPE
One buyer account per library and the observed supported page layouts. Imports are user-requested; opening a saved order refreshes it. Incomplete pages, login screens and totals that do not reconcile preserve the previous good version. Keep backups, including issued-code reservations, before moving computers, uninstalling or clearing your profile. Excel receives pasted values and floating pictures; direct workbook writing is not included.
```

## Permissions Justification

The production [manifest](manifest.json) requests no downloads, scripting, activeTab, cookies, history or clipboard-read permission. Version 2.6.0 adds no permission or external service. Material rules and translations run locally; private order descriptions are not uploaded or bundled.

| Permission                 | Type | Purpose                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| tabs                       | API  | Manage explicit imports and one reused tab for Open selected order; detect interrupted navigation and close only importer-created tabs |
| alarms                     | API  | Continue checkpointed steps while a requested import is active; no scheduled account crawler                                           |
| clipboardWrite             | API  | Copy product/preparation values, product pictures and generated labels after a user copy action                                        |
| unlimitedStorage           | API  | Retain orders, earlier versions, preparation data and pictures beyond small default limits; disk errors still report failures          |
| https://cbu01.alicdn.com/* | Host | Fetch and cache public product images, including original-size copies, without credentials                                             |

Packaged content scripts match these pages:

| Match                                                                     | Purpose                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| https://air.1688.com/app/ctf-page/trade-order-detail/index.html*          | Save/read complete orders, refresh saved orders, CSV controls                                                          |
| https://trade.1688.com/order/new_step_order_detail.htm*                   | Declared legacy route when its layout is supported                                                                     |
| https://air.1688.com/app/ctf-page/trade-order-list/buyer-order-list.html* | Order discovery and pagination                                                                                         |
| https://work.1688.com/*                                                   | Recognize the buyer workbench's directly rendered or embedded list; only mount controls when a supported list is found |

List scripts run in matching frames because the workbench hosts a separate list application. The toolbar opens a full extension page, with no popup. Test-only localhost and inspection permissions do not ship.

## Privacy & Data Use

**Does the extension handle user data? Yes, locally.**

| Data type                                                                                          | Handling                                                                | Off-device use                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Seller names, member/shop identifiers, purchase dates and order/product-entry IDs                  | Retained for identity, grouping and reconciliation                      | No developer upload                                                      |
| Prices, quantities, discounts, shipping, totals                                                    | Validated, retained with history and copied on request                  | No developer upload                                                      |
| Failed-import financial evidence and error details                                                 | Bounded local evidence retained with the import and exported on request | No automatic upload                                                      |
| Product text, variants, pictures                                                                   | Local database/cache                                                    | Image requests reach Alibaba CDN; imports make normal 1688 page requests |
| User notes/tags                                                                                    | Stored locally and included in backups                                  | No developer upload                                                      |
| Product groups, ordering, packing months, CE codes, variant/size mappings and restore checkpoints  | Stored separately from source orders; included in backups               | No developer upload                                                      |
| Spanish names, Chinese categories, materials, naming/size rules, label settings and selected logos | Stored locally; used to generate label images and included in backups   | No translation service or developer upload                               |
| Supported URLs/filters                                                                             | Explicit import and retained checkpoints                                | Requests to 1688 to load those pages                                     |
| Authentication information                                                                         | No password/token/cookie extraction                                     | Normal navigation uses the signed-in browser session                     |
| Recipient addresses, private communications, general history                                       | Not intentionally extracted or logged                                   | None                                                                     |
| Analytics, advertising, creditworthiness                                                           | Not collected for these purposes                                        | None                                                                     |

Orders, purchase dates, histories, notes, jobs, images and preparation data persist locally. Cached images are deduplicated by URL. Removing an order removes its versions and preparation; issued codes stay reserved and shared images may remain cached. Dismissing a job hides its notice while retaining the record. Version 2 backups include orders/history/notes/cached images, preparations with ordering and restore checkpoints, allocation counters, issued-code reservations and packing preferences with naming/size rules, not active jobs. Version 1 backups remain supported. Scoped packing backups retain every source row in their included orders and the reservation registry. Conflicting ownership rejects restore atomically; older backups cannot free issued codes. Import reports save queued order IDs, canonical detail URLs, statuses, expected row counts, errors, run counts and latest failed-read financial evidence on request. Evidence is bounded to 30 total fields, 25 product financial samples and aggregate amounts, with omitted counts. Invalid product discounts retain amount text capped at 160 characters and source row number. Reports exclude page HTML, product descriptions/images and session data. Retries retain the latest failure after success. No automatic upload occurs. Theme, purchase-month filters, Headers and copy-column choices are remembered separately in the browser; first-use defaults are dark, current purchase month and Headers off. Packing month starts at the current month whenever the workspace opens. Uninstalling or deleting the profile can delete the library. Clipboard data and saved files are subsequently controlled by the operating system and user.

Automatic naming uses bundled generic title rules and user-edited rules. Suggestions are retained locally on import, restore and existing-library updates, independently of source amounts and issued codes. Field authorship preserves manual names, including intentional blanks; unknown products remain unnamed. Size rules use local literal aliases and SKU/description measurements; product titles and article numbers do not establish sizes. Ambiguous assortments remain reviewable, and manual size mappings remain intact. Matching-order totals read saved financial data locally without extra network requests.

### Known limitations

- One buyer account per library; no automatic account detection.
- Unsupported layouts, installment totals, unknown adjustments and inconsistent totals report errors and preserve good saved data.
- Bulk images use standard-height Excel rows and are floating pictures. Custom row heights, other spreadsheet apps and native in-cell conversion need separate verification.
- Cached images work offline; unfetched images still need a connection. Large uncached copies can take time.
- Packing suggestions need review, especially missing structured SKU data or ambiguous sizes. Local naming rules are not general translation; unknown products stay unnamed and conflicting fields stay blank.
- Issued-code reservations belong to the local library and its backups; independent libraries do not coordinate numbering. Conflict ownership can be inspected, but old issued codes cannot be reassigned to another product or released by an older backup. Group moves stay within an order, and product moves stay within a group.
- Version 2.2.0 label pasting and Excel picture dimensions were verified; physical printing and hardware scanner use remain unverified. Direct workbook population is not implemented.
- Legacy CSV preserves item discounts; library copying also allocates order adjustments.
- Publisher details, public privacy policy, rights-cleared artwork, store screenshots and Dashboard settings remain unfinished. No store package or submission was created.
