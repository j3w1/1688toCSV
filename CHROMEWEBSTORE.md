### Known limitations

- One buyer account per library; no automatic account detection.
- Unsupported layouts, installment totals, unknown adjustments and inconsistent totals report errors and preserve good saved data.
- Bulk images use standard-height Excel rows and are floating pictures. Custom row heights, other spreadsheet apps and native in-cell conversion need separate verification.
- Cached images work offline; unfetched images still need a connection. Large uncached copies can take time.
- Packing suggestions need review, especially missing structured SKU data or ambiguous sizes. Local naming rules are not general translation; unknown products stay unnamed and conflicting fields stay blank.
- Current code assignments belong to the local library and its backups; independent libraries do not coordinate numbering. Changing or removing an assignment releases its old code, including previously copied labels. Undo/reset checkpoints do not reserve old codes, and restore checks current conflicts. Group moves stay within an order, and product moves stay within a group.
- Physical cartons support aggregate batches or exact numbered contents within one account/store/month, including same-purchase cross-code contents and eligible explicitly linked purchases. Planning defaults do not establish actual packing or confirmations. Separate container manifests are not modeled; batch-total weight is not prorated into partial confirmed weight.
- Version 2.2.0 label pasting and Excel picture dimensions were verified; physical printing and hardware scanner use remain unverified. Writing into an existing open workbook is not implemented; packing export creates a new XLSX.
- Legacy CSV preserves item discounts; library copying also allocates order adjustments.
- Publisher details, public privacy policy, rights-cleared artwork, store screenshots and Dashboard settings remain unfinished. No store package or submission was created.
